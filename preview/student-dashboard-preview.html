Profile & access

Role switcher is only a preview convenience. Real roles must be provisioned and verified securely.

Recommended live access model

Creator: platform-wide oversight. Principal: their school. Manager: granted scope. Teacher: assigned classes and subjects. Student: own read-only academic records.

School keys alone are not sufficient identity security. Use individual accounts, server-side authorization, MFA where appropriate and RLS.

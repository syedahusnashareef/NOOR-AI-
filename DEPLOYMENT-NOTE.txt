NOOR AI — Account-first school workspace

Backend project: NOOR-AI (dedicated Supabase project, India region).
Website: https://syedahusnashareef.github.io/NOOR-AI-/

Configured now:
- Frontend uses this project's Supabase URL and browser-safe publishable key.
- Student identity/profile schema, school directory, school/class/section records and teacher class assignments.
- Row Level Security is enabled on the deployed public tables.
- Student photo storage is private, limited to JPG/PNG/WebP files up to 3 MB.
- Role portal selector: Student, Teacher, Headmaster, Manager and Platform Creator. The authenticated profile role is checked after sign-in.

Not yet production-complete:
- No Platform Creator profile or school workspaces have been provisioned yet, so the student school selector will remain empty until a trusted Creator account creates school records.
- Staff accounts need trusted role provisioning; public student registration can never grant itself a management or Creator role.
- Attendance, marks, AI/OCR paper generation, tasks/notices, PDF reports and real email delivery are not yet fully connected/tested end to end.
- The first student registration and email verification flow must be tested with a real mailbox before entering real student/guardian information.

Never put Supabase service-role keys, database passwords or AI provider secrets in this public repository. Student photos use a private bucket and access policies.
